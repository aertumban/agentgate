How it works

Three steps, all on-chain. The contract re-checks your Agent ID live on every claim and every check-in — if the Agent ID leaves the wallet, the gate closes.

01

Hold an Agent ID

Claim your Agent ID (name.ai) in the Binance app under Wallet → Agent ID. That NFT in your wallet is your key — nothing else is required.

02

Claim your badge

Connect here and claim the AgentGate Badge (AGATE). It's soulbound: one per address, it can't be transferred or sold, and it holds no funds.

03

Check in & invite

Check in once a day to build your streak. Share your invite link — when another holder claims through it, the club grows holder-to-holder.
